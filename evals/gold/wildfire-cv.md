# Gold set — computer vision for wildfire and post-disaster damage

The recall half of chunk 2's done-check (`docs/design/chunk-02-scout.md` §6.4),
scoped to the acceptance-run topic the map records: post-disaster building damage
assessment from aerial and satellite imagery, plus computer vision and AI in
wildfires generally, including fire-spread prediction.

**Written by the user, from memory, without searching.** That constraint is the
whole point: a list produced by searching is not a test of whether search finds
things. If you catch yourself reaching for a browser, stop and write down what
you already know instead — a half-remembered first author and three words of a
title is a usable entry.

Eight entries is enough, twenty is better, and they do not have to be spread
evenly across the headings below. Leave a heading empty if nothing comes.

**Never edited after a scout run has been scored against it.** The moment this
file moves to match what the scout found, it stops measuring anything. Add to it
only before a run, never after one.

Resolved to Semantic Scholar ids by §6.2, through the retrieval script's `batch`
verb.
