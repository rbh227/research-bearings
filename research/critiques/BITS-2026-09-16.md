# Critique: BITS.md

## Findings

### The group's "one network trained end to end" thesis is contradicted by the same card's own description of BDANet as a two-stage pipeline

- Where: ## Groups — "Joint pre/post modelling on one benchmark — the thesis that damage is read from a pre/post image pair by one network trained end to end on xBD: gupta-2020-rescuenet, shen-2021-bdanet"; and ## Bits, "Damage is a property of a pre/post image pair that one network can be trained end to end to read" — "shen-2021-bdanet — its delta keeps the pair and the end-to-end frame and argues only about *how* the two images are combined"
- Objection: shen-2021-bdanet.md's own Delta section describes BDANet as "a two-stage framework (Stage 1: U-Net building segmentation from pre-disaster images only; Stage 2: a two-branch multi-scale U-Net with a cross-directional attention (CDA) module...)", and that same card's Kill experiment section explicitly contrasts BDANet against "single-stage, one-network approaches" (WNet, U-Net++), calling that comparison confounded precisely because BDANet is two-stage. The group and bit both assert shen-2021-bdanet "keeps... the end-to-end frame" of "one network," which is the opposite of what the card says about the paper's own architecture two sections later.
- Ask: Either reconcile with the card's Kill-experiment language (is BDANet one network or two stages?), or narrow the group thesis so it doesn't claim shen-2021-bdanet as an instance of single-network end-to-end training.

### A card explicitly marked unread is used as confirming evidence for a claim about what the paper does not state

- Where: ## Bits, "What a model is measured on is the standard split of one dataset, and which partition that is need not be stated" — "gupta-2020-rescuenet — `_not read_`: the card is a skim, and the introduction names the dataset without the protocol. That it is *unremarkable* for an introduction to omit the protocol is itself the assumption in force."
- Objection: gupta-2020-rescuenet.md's own Data and split field reads "_not read_ — the introduction names the dataset, not the protocol," and its Status line says the card is a skim where "Five card fields are `not read` because a skim reads the introduction only." That the paper omits the split protocol from its own results/experiments section was never checked — only the introduction was. Treating an unexamined field as evidence that the paper "need not state" the partition is an absence claim that names no surface checked (the results section, where the protocol would actually appear, was never read).
- Ask: Re-read gupta-2020-rescuenet past the introduction before counting it toward this bit, or drop it from the evidence list and note the bit rests on two cards, not three.

### The bit's second half ("which partition need not be stated") is carried by one card, not the three cited

- Where: ## Bits, "What a model is measured on is the standard split of one dataset, and which partition that is need not be stated" — "Cards: gupta-2020-rescuenet, shen-2021-bdanet, gupta-2019-xbd"
- Objection: gupta-2019-xbd is cited as evidence for this bit, but its own evidence line quotes the paper stating its split in full ("xBD is provided in train, test, and holdout splits in a 80/10/10% split ratio... meant to facilitate the xView 2 challenge"). That card is the opposite of "need not be stated" — it's the one paper in the group that states the partition precisely. Of the three cards listed, only shen-2021-bdanet actually supports "need not be stated" (gupta-2019-xbd contradicts it, gupta-2020-rescuenet is unread per the finding above). The bit lists three cards as if they jointly carried a two-part claim, when the second part is carried by exactly one.
- Ask: Split the bit into its two component claims, or state plainly that the "need not be stated" half has n=1 support among cards read in full.

### An evidence quote splices two different papers' techniques into one undifferentiated pair, losing which paper does which

- Where: ## Bits, "Damage is a property of a pre/post image pair that one network can be trained end to end to read" — "a single fixed operation… concatenation or subtraction"
- Objection: shen-2021-bdanet.md's "Bit flipped" section attributes concatenation to Weber et al. and subtraction (difference) to RescueNet specifically: "concatenation (Weber et al. [12]) or subtraction (RescueNet [14])." The ellipsis in BITS.md collapses this into an undifferentiated "concatenation or subtraction," which reads as if either technique could describe RescueNet (the very card this bit pairs shen-2021-bdanet against), when the card is clear RescueNet is the subtraction case only.
- Ask: Quote the full attributed phrase, or name RescueNet's method explicitly rather than leaving "concatenation or subtraction" unassigned.
